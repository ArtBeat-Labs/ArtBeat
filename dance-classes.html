Children usually start with Bollywood or a Zumba-style batch and move into Garba and stage choreography as they grow. Dance class questions

I have never danced before. Can I still join?expand_more

Yes. Every dance batch at ART BEAT takes complete beginners, and a large part of each session is built up step by step. Nobody is asked to know the routine before turning up.

Do you teach Zumba?expand_more

Yes, Zumba is one of the styles taught at the studio, alongside Bollywood and Garba. Message us on WhatsApp at +91 93363 33394 for current Zumba batch timings.

Do you teach kids as well as adults?expand_more

Yes. ART BEAT teaches dance to children, teenagers and adults, in age-appropriate groups so nobody is out of place.

Do you take Garba classes before the festival season?expand_more

Garba is taught through the year at ART BEAT, which usually means batches are already running when the season approaches. Ask us when to join so you are placed in a batch that finishes in time.

Is there group dancing or only private coaching?expand_more

Both. Most students join group classes, and one-to-one coaching is available if you want extra focus, a faster pace, or preparation for a performance.

What should I wear and bring to dance class?expand_more

Comfortable clothing you can move in and indoor shoes. Ask us when you enrol and we will confirm exactly what the studio needs.
