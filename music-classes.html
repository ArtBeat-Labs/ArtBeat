Music Classes in Lucknow — Piano, Guitar, Tabla & Singing

Looking for music classes in Lucknow? ART BEAT teaches piano, guitar, tabla, harmonium and singing at Eldeco Saubhagyam Club House, Sector 9, Vrindavan Colony. Lessons run in small batches for children and adults, absolute beginners welcome, taught by Ayush Kumar and Rishabh. The studio is open Monday to Saturday 10 AM to 8 PM and Sunday 10 AM to 6 PM.

Instruments and voice we teach

piano

Piano

Posture, reading, scales and chords, building up to playing full pieces. Taught on keyboard and piano.

music_note

Guitar

Holding position, fingerstyle and strumming, chord progressions and rhythm. Popular with teens and adults.

album

Tabla

Bayan and dayan, bols, taal and the first compositions. The studio's most popular instrument for children.

graphic_eq

Harmonium

Hand position, finger movement and accompaniment patterns, often learned alongside tabla.

mic

Singing

Breath control, pitch and voice training, then songs the student actually wants to sing.

groups

Music fusion

Art and music combined sessions, where students make visual work to a rhythm they have built themselves.

Music class questions

Can I start music lessons with no musical background at all?expand_more

Yes. The studio teaches complete beginners, and lessons start from how to hold the instrument or how to breathe and match a note. You do not need to read music or have played before.

Do I need my own instrument to join?expand_more

Ask us when you enquire. We can tell you whether instruments are available at the studio for practice, and what is sensible to buy or rent if you want your own.

Do you teach tabla and Indian classical music?expand_more

Yes. Tabla and harmonium are taught alongside piano and guitar, with tabla especially popular with kids in the Vrindavan Colony and Aliganj area.

Do you teach singing separately, or only instruments?expand_more

Singing and vocal training are taught as their own course as well as alongside instruments. Beginners who are not sure which instrument to start with often begin with voice.

What ages do you take for music lessons?expand_more

ART BEAT teaches all ages and levels. Children usually start on tabla, keyboard or voice, while adults commonly take piano, guitar or singing. Message us on WhatsApp at +91 93363 33394 for the right batch.

Can siblings or two students from one family share a batch?expand_more

Tell us when you enquire and we will tell you what is possible with current batch sizes and timings.
