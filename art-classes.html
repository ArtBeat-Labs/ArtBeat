Painting & drawing in Lucknow

Art Classes in Lucknow — Painting, Sketching & Pencil Shading

Looking for art classes in Lucknow? ART BEAT is an art studio at Eldeco Saubhagyam Club House, Sector 9, Vrindavan Colony, run by founder and art instructor Shefali. We teach oil painting, acrylic painting, pencil shading and sketching to children and adults, from complete beginner to advanced. Classes run in small batches through the week, and the studio is open Monday to Saturday 10 AM to 8 PM and Sunday 10 AM to 6 PM.

What we teach in the art studio

palette

Oil painting

Layering, brush control, blending and working on canvas and board. A favourite with older students and adults.

brush

Acrylic painting

Quick-drying acrylics for bold colour work, landscapes and abstract pieces. Great for teenagers who lose patience with slow-drying oils.

gesture

Pencil shading

Our most popular course for teenagers. Light control, gradients, textures and realistic portrait shading on paper.

edit

Sketching & drawing

Observation, proportion and line work. The foundation every other art form at the studio is built on.

face

Portrait work

Drawing and shading faces and figures, from simple shapes through to a finished realistic study.

colorize

Colour theory

Colour mixing, warm and cool, value and composition, so students can paint what they actually see.

Art class questions

Do I need any previous experience to join an art class?expand_more

No. ART BEAT teaches complete beginners as well as students who have been painting for years. Batches are grouped by level so you are not taught material far above or below where you are.

How old should my child be to start art classes?expand_more

Children can start young, and most beginners here are of school age. For the exact starting age and the right batch, message us on WhatsApp at +91 93363 33394 and we will advise you.

Do I need to buy my own paints, brushes and canvas?expand_more

You will need art materials to practise between classes. Ask us when you enquire and we will tell you exactly which materials your batch needs and where they can be bought in Lucknow.

Which art forms do you teach?expand_more

Oil painting, acrylic painting, pencil shading and portrait work, and free-hand sketching and drawing. We also cover colour theory and composition as the students progress.

Do you take adults as well as children?expand_more

Yes. ART BEAT teaches all ages and levels. Several adults come for pencil shading, sketching and oil painting in the evening batches.

Where exactly is the studio in Lucknow?expand_more

The studio is at Eldeco Saubhagyam Club House, Sector 9, Vrindavan Colony, near LPS School, Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh 226029. It is easy to reach from Hazratganj, Aliganj, Munshipulia and Gomti Nagar.

How do I book a batch or ask about fees?expand_more

Send us a WhatsApp message at +91 93363 33394 or call the same number. We will share current batch timings and fees, and arrange a visit to the studio.
