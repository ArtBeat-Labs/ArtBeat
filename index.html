We are a passionate art and music studio where creativity comes to life. Learn and explore a wide range of art forms, including oil painting, pencil shading, sketching, acrylic painting, and more. Whether you're discovering your passion or looking to take your creativity to the next level, our studio provides a welcoming and inspiring space to learn, create, and express yourself.

Good to Know

Questions, Answered

Where is the studio?+

Eldeco Saubhagyam Club House, Sector 9, Vrindavan Colony (near LPS School), Lucknow 226029. Get directions

What are the hours?+

Monday–Saturday 10 AM–8 PM, Sunday 10 AM–6 PM. Classes run on alternate days per batch — with multiple batches, the studio is open every day.

What can I learn?+

Art: oil, acrylic, pencil shading, sketching. Music: piano, guitar, tabla, singing. Dance: group classes and individual coaching. All ages and levels, beginner to advanced.

Who teaches here?+

Shefali (founder) and Ankit teach art. Ayush and Rishabh teach music.

Tell me about the competition+

The ART BEAT Open Competition runs 2 October from 11:00 AM at Eldeco Saubhagyam Club — art, music and dance, open to all ages and levels. Details and registration
